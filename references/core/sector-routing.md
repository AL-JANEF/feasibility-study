# Sector Routing

## Why routing is mandatory
The term "feasibility study" does not define a single analytical template.
Sector, subsector, business model, jurisdiction, stage, and financing determine the
required analyses.

## Classification dimensions
- Sector
- Subsector
- Product/service
- B2B / B2C / B2G
- Revenue mechanism
- Asset-light / asset-heavy
- Working-capital intensity
- Regulatory intensity
- Safety/clinical/environmental criticality
- Jurisdiction
- New venture / expansion / acquisition / market entry
- Equity / debt / project finance / PPP / grant

## Starter sector map
Use the files in `references/sectors/` as starter packs.

### Healthcare and life sciences
Hospital, clinic, laboratory, pharmacy, home care, digital health, medical device,
IVD, pharmaceutical, biotech.

### Industrial and resources
Discrete manufacturing, process manufacturing, food manufacturing, chemicals,
pharmaceutical manufacturing, agriculture, livestock, aquaculture, energy, mining,
quarrying.

### Digital and services
SaaS, enterprise software, marketplace, e-commerce, professional services, managed
services, FinTech, contracting, EPC, O&M.

### Asset-heavy and consumer
Real estate, hospitality, restaurant/cafe, education, transport/logistics,
warehousing, infrastructure/PPP, franchise, retail/wholesale.

## Dynamic sector-pack synthesis
If the precise subsector is not covered, do not use the generic core alone.

Before Stage 02, research and write a temporary sector pack containing:

1. Sector definition and project boundary.
2. Demand unit and demand drivers.
3. Revenue unit and pricing convention.
4. Capacity unit and utilization logic.
5. Design/technical basis.
6. Critical inputs and supply chain.
7. Core CAPEX categories.
8. Core OPEX/COGS categories.
9. Working-capital mechanics.
10. Sector-specific accounting/revenue-recognition issues.
11. Competent regulator(s) and approvals.
12. Safety, environment, quality, clinical, or professional standards.
13. Sector KPIs and benchmark definitions.
14. Typical implementation lead times.
15. Top sector failure modes.
16. Sector-specific sensitivity variables.
17. Sector-specific red-team questions.

Use authoritative sources. A dynamically synthesized sector pack is part of the evidence
base and must cite its sources.
