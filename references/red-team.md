# Independent Red Team

Attack the completed case using independent contexts/agents where available.

Required lenses:
- Market skeptic: real demand, budget, competitor response, switching costs, TAM boundary, WTP.
- Financial skeptic: revenue identity, missing cost, working capital, funding, downside survival, sensitivity.
- Operator: staffing, capacity, suppliers/logistics, schedule, critical path, founder bandwidth.
- Technical/regulatory skeptic: unknowns, architecture/data dependencies, security/privacy, permits/licenses.

Objections use severity `BLOCKER`, `IMPORTANT`, `OPTIONAL` and disposition `FIXED`, `ACCEPTED_RISK`,
`REJECTED_WITH_EVIDENCE`, `OPEN`.

A BLOCKER cannot disappear from the final decision.
