# Risk & Pre-Mortem

Cover relevant market, competitive, technical, operational, financial, regulatory/legal, supply-chain,
people/execution, data/security/privacy, and reputation risks.

For each risk capture cause, event, consequence, likelihood, impact, evidence, early-warning indicator,
mitigation, contingency, owner and residual risk.

Run a pre-mortem: assume the project failed, trace the primary cause, enabling conditions, missed warning,
bad decision and earliest detectable signal. Convert it into measurable early warnings and kill/pivot criteria.
