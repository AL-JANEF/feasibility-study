# Reporting Standard

Lead the executive memo with decision question, state, confidence, capital/time at risk, 3–5 load-bearing
findings, blockers/conditions, key financial outputs, next irreversible decision, and validation actions.

Full report: scope/evidence quality, market, competition, technical, operational, regulatory, business model,
financial model, scenarios/sensitivity, risk/pre-mortem, resources, validation, red team, reconciliation,
decision state/conditions, sources and assumptions.

Answer first. Separate fact from inference. Use ranges. Carry unresolved blockers to the executive summary.
Never call a model audited unless a qualified audit actually occurred.
