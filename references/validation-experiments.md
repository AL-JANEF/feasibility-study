# Validation Experiments

Critical unknowns that can flip the decision become tests.

Each experiment records assumption ID, hypothesis, cheapest credible test, sample/audience, duration, cost,
metric, pass threshold, fail threshold, ambiguous zone, and decision changed by result.

Prefer behavior: paid pilot, pre-order/deposit, qualified landing-page conversion, procurement interview,
technical spike, supplier quote, permit pre-consultation, production trial, channel test, pricing test.

Write 3–7 measurable kill/pivot criteria that the team is actually willing to obey.
