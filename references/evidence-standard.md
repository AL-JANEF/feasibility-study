# Evidence Standard

Every load-bearing statement is one of:

- `FACT` — directly supported by a source or supplied record.
- `ESTIMATE` — calculated from stated inputs/formula.
- `ASSUMPTION` — intentionally unverified input used to make progress.
- `JUDGMENT` — analytical interpretation based on cited evidence.

Never label an inference as FACT.

## Source hierarchy

1. `PRIMARY` — government/regulator, filings, official company disclosure, direct price list,
   signed contract, measured first-party product data.
2. `AUTHORITATIVE_SECONDARY` — recognized industry body, bank/economic institution,
   peer-reviewed research, established analyst publication with disclosed method.
3. `REPUTABLE_SECONDARY` — established business/technology media and credible specialist research.
4. `COMMUNITY` — reviews/forums/social; useful for voice-of-customer, weak for hard totals.
5. `UNKNOWN`.

Search-result snippets are discovery leads, not final evidence.

## Confidence

`HIGH` requires strong direct evidence or convergent high-quality sources.
`MEDIUM` fits derived or incomplete evidence. `LOW` marks weak proxy, stale data,
uncertain boundary or assumption-heavy derivation. `UNKNOWN` is not decision-ready.

Every material number exposes units, geography, period, source/input IDs, formula if derived,
and uncertainty/range where material.

When sources disagree, verify metric, geography, period and category boundary before choosing.
Preserve contradictions until reconciled.
