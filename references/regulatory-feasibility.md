# Regulatory & Jurisdiction Feasibility

Using current official sources identify entity formation/ownership requirements, activity/license,
sector regulator, municipal/site permits, tax/zakat/VAT/customs implications, labor/localization,
privacy/cyber/data residency, accessibility/consumer rules, environment/HSE, product standards,
import/export controls, foreign-investment requirements, sequence, dependencies, cost and lead time.

Use a matrix: Requirement | Authority | Applies? | Evidence | Lead time | Cost | Dependency | Status.

Use `UNKNOWN` when applicability depends on unresolved facts. Official rules outrank commentary.
A regulatory issue is a blocker if lawful operation cannot fit the decision horizon or destroys the economics.
