# Operational Feasibility

Assess process design, staffing, capacity, suppliers, procurement lead times, facilities/site,
utilities, logistics, QA, maintenance/support, SLAs, seasonality, inventory, working capital,
business continuity, and single points of dependency.

For industrial/manufacturing projects additionally model nameplate vs practical capacity, yield/scrap,
raw materials and FX exposure, MOQ/lead time, utilities intensity, equipment uptime, QA/certification,
warehousing/logistics, ramp-up, commissioning, and OEM/spare-parts dependency.

Revenue cannot exceed operational capacity without an explicit expansion assumption.
