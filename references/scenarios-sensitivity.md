# Scenarios & Sensitivity

Scenarios tell coherent stories; sensitivity isolates one driver.

Standard/investment-grade uses DOWNSIDE, BASE and UPSIDE. Choose driver changes from actual risks,
not decorative +/-10%. Test launch delay, price, volume ramp, CAC, churn, yield, material cost,
collections, permit delay, payroll, utilization as relevant.

Find breakpoints: at what driver value does the project stop meeting the success threshold?
Acknowledge correlated drivers rather than changing them independently without explanation.
