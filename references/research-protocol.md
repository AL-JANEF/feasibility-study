# Research Protocol

For each dimension define the decision-relevant question, evidence required, preferred source
classes, search strategy, stop condition, and what would falsify the current hypothesis.

Fan out research by dimension: market size, customer demand/WTP, competitors/pricing,
regulation, cost/suppliers/labor, distribution, technology, and financing/support where relevant.
Reconcile centrally.

For load-bearing claims run a hostile second pass whose job is to disprove the headline market
size, top competitor claims, WTP anchor, critical regulation, cost driver, and technical dependency.

Research is sufficient when another ordinary source is unlikely to change the decision. Continue
when a missing fact could flip it.

If current web evidence is required and unavailable, mark the finding `UNKNOWN`; do not silently
substitute model memory for current market or regulatory facts.
