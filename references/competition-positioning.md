# Competition & Positioning

Map competition by the customer's job, including direct rivals, adjacent products, manual process,
spreadsheet/email/WhatsApp workaround, outsourced service, and status quo.

For important competitors capture target customer, job solved, offering, pricing/value metric,
distribution, traction evidence, strengths, evidence-backed weaknesses, switching costs, likely
response, and whether differentiation is a moat, temporary edge, or table stakes.

Use reviews/forums to extract recurring pains, requested capabilities, churn reasons, exact customer
language, budget objections and migration triggers.

Position around a falsifiable advantage, not a generic feature list.
