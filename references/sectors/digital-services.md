# Sector Pack — Digital, Software, Platforms, Services

## SaaS / Enterprise Software
Demand: ICP count, budget owner, replacement trigger, procurement cycle, sales cycle,
pipeline conversion, willingness to pay, implementation friction.
Revenue: bookings, go-live, ACV/ARPA, ARR/MRR, renewals, expansion, churn, services.
Capacity: sales reps/quota/ramp, implementation consultants, support capacity, cloud.
Costs: cloud/support COGS, implementation labor, sales commission, R&D/product, security,
compliance, customer success.
Working capital/accounting: prepaid annual billing, AR, deferred revenue/contract liabilities,
revenue recognition.
KPIs: ARR, MRR, ACV, new ARR, GRR, NRR, logo churn, CAC, CAC payback, LTV, gross margin,
burn multiple, concentration.
Red team: unsupported conversion, unrealistic rep productivity, ignored implementation
bottleneck, low retention, underpriced support/security, cash/revenue confusion.

## Marketplace
Demand and supply must both be feasible.
Drivers: active supply, active demand, geographic/category density, match/fill rate, orders,
GMV, take rate, repeat rate, incentives, payment cost, refund/fraud/chargeback.
KPIs: GMV, take rate, contribution/order, fill rate, repeat, liquidity, CAC by side.
Red team: subsidy-dependent liquidity, weak repeat, thin contribution margin.

## E-commerce
Traffic, conversion, AOV, gross margin, CAC, repeat, fulfillment, last mile, payment method,
returns, COD, inventory turns, stockouts, markdowns, marketplace/platform fees.
KPIs: contribution/order, CAC, repeat, return rate, gross margin, inventory turns.

## Professional / Managed Services
Billable FTE, utilization, bill rate, project mix, backlog, delivery capacity, subcontracting,
bench time, DSO, project overruns.
KPIs: utilization, revenue/FTE, gross margin/project, backlog coverage, DSO.

## FinTech / Financial Services
First classify the regulated activity.
Research license/sandbox, regulatory capital, safeguarding/client money, AML/KYC,
cybersecurity, fraud, consumer protection, settlement, liquidity, partnerships.
Drivers: active customers, transaction volume/TPV, take rate, spread/fee, loss/fraud,
chargebacks, cost/transaction.
