# Sector Pack — Real Estate, Hospitality, Consumer, Education, Logistics, PPP

## Real Estate Development
Title/zoning, highest and best use, land cost, GFA/NLA, unit mix, design/construction,
phasing, permits, sales/lease absorption, price/rent, incentives, collections, operating
costs, NOI, exit yield/cap rate, financing drawdown, escrow/pre-sales where applicable.
KPIs: GDV, development margin, NOI, cap rate, occupancy, absorption, LTC/LTV, DSCR,
project/equity IRR, residual land value.

## Hotels / Hospitality
Destination/source-market demand, seasonality, rooms/keys, room nights, occupancy, ADR,
RevPAR, F&B/events/other revenue, OTA commissions, operator/brand fees, staffing,
FF&E, pre-opening, reserve for replacement, ramp-up.
KPIs: occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, TRevPAR, GOP/GOPPAR, cost/occupied room.

## Restaurant / Cafe
Site/footfall/delivery catchment, seats, turns, dayparts, average check, covers/orders,
menu mix, food cost, waste, labor, rent, delivery commissions, kitchen capacity,
hygiene/food safety.
KPIs: average check, covers, table turn, food cost %, labor %, prime cost, rent/sales,
contribution/order.

## Education / Training
Catchment, learner population, capacity, admissions funnel, enrollment ramp, tuition,
discount/scholarship, retention, teachers/instructors, student-teacher ratio, facilities,
curriculum/accreditation, transport, seasonality.
KPIs: enrollment, utilization, revenue/student, teacher cost/student, retention.

## Transport / Logistics / Warehousing
Routes/lanes, shipment volume, fleet/warehouse capacity, utilization/load factor,
empty miles/backhaul, fuel, tolls, maintenance, drivers, warehouse throughput,
pick/pack, damage/loss, OTIF/SLA.
KPIs: cost/km, revenue/km, load factor, empty miles, fleet utilization, OTIF,
warehouse occupancy/throughput.

## Infrastructure / PPP
Service need/demand, technical options, lifecycle CAPEX/OPEX, E&S, economic cost-benefit,
affordability, bankability, risk allocation, payment mechanism, tariff/availability
payments, government support/fiscal commitments, procurement structure, handback.
Metrics: ENPV/EIRR when public economic analysis applies, project/equity IRR, DSCR,
LLCR, value-for-money.

## Retail / Wholesale / Franchise
Traffic/channel demand, conversion, AOV, SKU/category margin, supplier terms, inventory,
shrinkage, markdowns, returns, seasonality, DIO/DPO/DSO, GMROI.
Franchise additionally: franchise fee, royalty, marketing fund, unit economics,
franchisee payback, territory, support burden, closure rate.
