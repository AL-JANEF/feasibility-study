# Sector Pack — Industrial, Food, Agriculture, Energy, Mining

## Manufacturing
Define product specs and process flow.
Capacity: nameplate -> effective -> scheduled -> yield-adjusted saleable output.
Research equipment, line balance, cycle time, yield, scrap/rework, OEE, shifts, labor,
BOM/raw materials, utilities, maintenance/spares, QA/QC, warehousing, logistics, ramp-up,
environmental/safety controls.
Financial drivers: units, utilization, yield, unit selling price, BOM cost, energy/unit,
labor/unit, fixed manufacturing overhead, maintenance, inventory.
KPIs: capacity utilization, OEE, yield, scrap %, throughput, unit cost, inventory days.

## Food Manufacturing
Add food safety/HACCP as applicable, recipe/BOM yield, shelf life, cold chain, batch
traceability, packaging, expiry/waste, recall capability, sanitation, raw-material seasonality.

## Agriculture / Livestock / Aquaculture
Land/water/soil/climate, biological cycle, yield, mortality, feed conversion, genetics,
biosecurity/disease, irrigation, stocking density, harvest survival, post-harvest loss,
cold chain, seasonal production, commodity price, insurance.
KPIs: yield/ha, cost/kg, water/unit, mortality, FCR, cycle length.

## Energy / Renewables
Resource assessment, site, capacity, energy yield, capacity factor, degradation,
interconnection/grid, EPC, PPA/tariff, curtailment, storage, availability, O&M,
decommissioning.
KPIs: MWh, capacity factor, availability, LCOE, curtailment, project/equity IRR, DSCR.

## Mining / Quarrying
Resource/reserve basis, grade, recovery, mine plan, strip ratio, metallurgy/process,
throughput, commodity price, royalties, logistics, water/power, tailings/waste,
permitting, closure/rehabilitation, sustaining CAPEX.
KPIs: recovery, grade, throughput, cash cost/AISC where applicable, mine life, NPV/IRR.
