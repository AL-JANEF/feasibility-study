# Sector Pack — Healthcare and Life Sciences

## Hospital / Clinic / Medical Center / Laboratory
Market: catchment population, disease/service need, referrals, competing capacity,
payer mix, insurance network/contracting, reimbursement/tariffs, private-pay demand.
Capacity: beds, rooms, chairs, theatres, lab analyzers, clinic sessions, physician/nurse/
technician hours, equipment availability.
Operations: staffing ratios, credentialing, scheduling, consumables, pharmacy/lab,
infection prevention, clinical governance, medical records, emergency capability,
medical waste, maintenance/calibration.
Revenue: visits/procedures/bed-days/tests x utilization x payer/reimbursement mix.
Working capital: insurer receivables, denials, collection lag, inventory/consumables.
KPIs: occupancy, ALOS, bed turnover, revenue/bed, visits/session, procedure margin,
denial rate, DSO, clinician/equipment utilization.
Regulation: facility licensing, professional licensing, accreditation, infection control,
radiology/lab/pharmacy requirements, data/privacy, insurance/reimbursement as applicable.

## Medical Device / IVD
Clinical unmet need; device/IVD classification; intended use; regulatory pathway;
QMS/design controls; verification/validation; clinical/performance evidence; manufacturing
validation; sterilization where applicable; shelf life; packaging; supplier controls;
authorization/registration; procurement/reimbursement; installation/service; post-market
surveillance; vigilance/recall.
Economics: unit/consumable model, installed base, utilization, service cost, yield, warranty,
distributor margin.

## Pharmaceutical / Biotech
Target product profile; unmet need; IP/exclusivity; preclinical; clinical phases;
probability of technical/regulatory success; CMC; GMP; stability; batch yield;
manufacturing/tech transfer; submission/approval; pharmacovigilance; market access;
pricing/reimbursement; launch sequence; cold chain where applicable.
Financial model: milestone-based pre-revenue burn, success/failure branches,
risk-adjusted NPV where appropriate, licensing/royalty economics.

## Digital Health
Combine software/SaaS economics with healthcare regulation, clinical claims, privacy,
interoperability, provider/payer workflow, and evidence requirements. Do not treat it as
ordinary SaaS if diagnosis/treatment/medical-device claims may apply.
