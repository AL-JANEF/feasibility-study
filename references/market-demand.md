# Market & Demand

Market size and demand are separate questions.

## Demand evidence stack

Seek evidence from observed spend on alternatives, competitor pricing/paid plans, customer reviews,
procurement activity, search/community demand, interviews/pilots/pre-orders/LOIs, switching behavior,
and mandatory regulatory/operational forces.

## TAM / SAM / SOM

Bottom-up first. Examples:

`reachable buyers × annual spend per buyer`

`annual transactions × feasible take rate`

`capacity × utilization × price`

State every input and evidence ID. Use top-down category figures only as a boundary check.
If top-down and bottom-up differ materially, investigate category definitions, geography,
B2B/B2C mixing, GMV vs vendor revenue, list vs realized price, and forecast vs actual.

Behavior outranks stated willingness to pay. Paid behavior outranks survey enthusiasm.
