# Business Model

Map the business as a causal system: customer/payer, problem/job, value, revenue streams, value metric,
price, volume driver, acquisition, conversion, retention/repeat, delivery capacity, variable-cost driver,
fixed costs, working capital, and key dependencies.

Write the revenue identity before values. Examples:

`Revenue = customers × transactions/customer × price`
`Marketplace revenue = GMV × take rate`
`SaaS MRR = active paid accounts × ARPA`
`Factory revenue = saleable units × realized price`

Model multiple revenue streams separately. Use metrics appropriate to the model; do not apply SaaS
metrics to factories merely because they are familiar.
