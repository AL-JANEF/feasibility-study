# Saudi Arabia Jurisdiction Profile

Use this as a live-research map, not a static statement of law.

**Always verify the current rule, effective date, activity, entity type, location and sector on
current official Saudi sources. Do not hardcode tax rates, Saudization percentages, fees,
ownership limits or licensing timelines.**

## Primary-source starting points

Depending on the project, check:

- Saudi Business Center / Ministry of Commerce — establishment and business-service pathways.
- GASTAT — official economic, demographic, labor and industry statistics.
- ZATCA — zakat, tax, VAT, customs, invoicing and related rules.
- Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development / Qiwa — labor/localization requirements.
- Ministry of Investment — foreign-investment requirements when relevant.
- Ministry of Industry and Mineral Resources — industrial licensing/sector requirements.
- MODON — industrial cities, land/facility options and industrial ecosystem information.
- Saudi Industrial Development Fund (SIDF) — industrial financing/support and eligibility.
- Balady / municipal authorities — site/municipal permits where applicable.
- Sector regulators for finance, communications/technology, health/food, transport, energy,
  capital markets or other regulated activities.
- SDAIA / NCA / other competent authorities where privacy, data or cybersecurity obligations apply.

Confirm the current competent authority; responsibilities can change.

## Saudi-specific feasibility questions

### Commercial / ownership
- Correct activity code and legal entity?
- Local vs foreign ownership considerations?
- Any special professional/sector license?
- Can planned activities coexist under one entity/license?

### Labor
- Required workforce composition?
- Current localization rules for exact activity/occupation?
- Visa/work-permit implications?
- Wage/benefit/onboarding cost assumptions?

### Tax and customs
- Applicable tax/zakat treatment?
- VAT/invoicing obligations?
- Import duties/customs and product classification?
- Withholding/cross-border implications if relevant?

### Industrial
For factories/manufacturing also research industrial license pathway, land/factory location,
utilities/logistics, environmental/municipal/Civil Defense requirements as applicable, product
conformity/standards, financing/support eligibility, local content, machinery/raw-material imports,
and commissioning/inspection sequence.

Official Saudi sources outrank blogs, consultants, social posts and memory for current rules.
